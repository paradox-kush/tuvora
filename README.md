# ▶ Tuvora

**A free, open-source player for your own M3U, Xtream Codes and Stalker playlists and your Jellyfin and Emby servers — on Android TV, Fire TV, Apple TV, phones, Mac, Windows and Linux.**

Bring your own playlist or media server. Tuvora gives it a fast interface with posters, a real EPG guide, a Sports centre, and free sync across your devices.

**Website & downloads: https://tuvora.co**

</div>

---

> **Tuvora is a player.** It does not provide, host, or sell any channels, movies, or series. You play content from playlists, subscriptions and media servers **you already have**.

## 📥 Get the app

Everything is linked from **https://tuvora.co** — the download row covers every platform:

- **Android phone / tablet:** [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tuvora.mobile), or the full version (with add-ons) at https://tuvora.co/apk

- **Mac:** https://tuvora.co/mac (Apple Silicon) · https://tuvora.co/mac-intel

## 🐞 Report a bug or request a feature

- **Something broken?** → [Open an issue](https://github.com/paradox-kush/tuvora/issues/new/choose)
- **Playlist / source won't work?** → use the *"Playlist / source won't work"* issue form
- **Idea or feature request?** → [Open an issue](https://github.com/paradox-kush/tuvora/issues/new/choose)

> 🔒 **Never paste your full server URL, username, or password** in a public issue or post. Redact them (e.g. `http://REDACTED:8080`).
