# ▶ Tuvora

**A free IPTV player for your own M3U, Xtream Codes and Stalker playlists — on phone, TV, Mac and Windows.**

Bring your own playlist. Tuvora gives it a fast interface with posters, a real EPG guide, a Sports centre, and free sync across your devices.

**Website & downloads: https://tuvora.co**

</div>

---

> **Tuvora is a player.** It does not provide, host, or sell any channels, movies, or series. You play content from playlists and subscriptions **you already have**.

## 📥 Get the app

Everything is linked from **https://tuvora.co** — the download row covers every platform:

- **Android phone / tablet:** Google Play open testing (join https://groups.google.com/g/tuvora-testers first, then https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.tuvora.mobile) — or the direct APK at https://tuvora.co/apk
- **Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV:** https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.tuvora.tv, or sideload with Downloader code **5040595** (https://tuvora.co/tv)
- **iPhone / iPad:** TestFlight — https://testflight.apple.com/join/XDc6gVXc
- **Mac:** https://tuvora.co/mac (Apple Silicon) · https://tuvora.co/mac-intel
- **Windows:** https://tuvora.co/windows

Releases with changelogs: [TuvoraMobile](https://github.com/paradox-kush/TuvoraMobile/releases) · [TuvoraTV](https://github.com/paradox-kush/TuvoraTV/releases) · [TuvoraDesktop](https://github.com/paradox-kush/TuvoraDesktop/releases)

## 🐞 Report a bug or request a feature
