# ▶ Tuvora

**A beautiful media player for your own live TV, movies, and series — on your phone and your TV.**

Bring your own **M3U / Xtream Codes / Stalker** playlist. Tuvora gives it a gorgeous, fast interface with posters, an EPG guide, a Sports centre, and sync across your devices.

</div>

---

> **Tuvora is a media player.** It does not provide, host, or sell any channels, movies, or series. You play content from playlists and subscriptions **you already have**.

## 📥 Get the app (closed beta)

Tuvora is in closed testing on Google Play right now. To install:

1. **Join the tester group:** https://groups.google.com/g/tuvora-testers
2. Then open the opt-in link for your device:
   - **Phone / tablet:** https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.tuvora.mobile
   - **Android TV / Google TV:** https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.tuvora.tv
3. Tap **"Download it on Google Play"** and install.

> Beta spots help us reach production faster — thank you for testing! 🙏
